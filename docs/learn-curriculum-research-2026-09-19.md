# SukuuNova Learn curriculum research notes — 2026-09-19

These notes explain the source-backed structure used by the Learn catalogue. They are implementation guidance, not a claim that SukuuNova reproduces every institution's official handbook.

## Ghana school structure and SHS pathways

NaCCA describes Ghana's pre-tertiary phases as KG 1–2, Primary 1–6, JHS 1–3 and SHS 1–3:
- https://nacca.gov.gh/curriculum/

NaCCA's current secondary subject-combination guidance says learners may be externally assessed in 7–9 subjects and that schools should contextualise combinations based on capacity and focus:
- https://nacca.gov.gh/subject-combination-guidelines-secondary-education/

Product consequence: SukuuNova exposes familiar learner-facing pathways such as General Science, General Arts, Business, Visual Arts, Home Economics, Agriculture, Technical and STEM, but treats them as flexible navigation groupings rather than pretending they are rigid national programme rules.

## University programme breadth

The programme catalogue is programme-first and institution-neutral because exact course codes, semester order and specialisations differ between universities.

Official sources used to establish breadth and common programme families include:

- University of Ghana programme catalogue and admissions:
  - https://www.ug.edu.gh/programme-catalogue
  - https://admissions.ug.edu.gh/undergraduate/general-guidelines
- KNUST 2026/2027 undergraduate programme list:
  - https://www.knust.edu.gh/index.php/announcements/undergraduate-admissions/admission-candidates-undergraduate-degree-programmes-20262027-academic-year
- University of Cape Coast undergraduate programmes:
  - https://ucc.edu.gh/main/applicants-and-students/undergraduate-programmes
- University for Development Studies programmes:
  - https://uds.edu.gh/academics/programmes/

These official catalogues support broad programme families including Computer Science, IT, Computer Engineering, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Law, Economics, Business, Accounting, Finance, Marketing, HRM, Supply Chain, Engineering disciplines, Architecture, Psychology, Political Science, Sociology, Communication, Public Health, Biomedical Science, Biochemistry, Actuarial Science, Statistics, Education, Agriculture and Hospitality/Tourism.

University of Ghana explicitly uses Level 100–400 progression for many undergraduate programmes, while professional programmes may run longer. KNUST and UDS official listings also show longer professional programmes such as medicine and pharmacy.

## Truthfulness rule

SukuuNova must not label its common course maps as the exact curriculum of a named university unless that institution's current official handbook/course structure has been verified. The UI therefore says that exact course codes and semester order vary by institution.

Question readiness is separate from catalogue breadth. A programme may have a broad mapped course structure while only some courses have published practice. The product should keep expanding question depth without substituting unrelated questions.
