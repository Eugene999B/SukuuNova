# Discovery and learning quality
## Shipped paths
- /explore: three deterministic educational models, reflection, browser-local projects, JSON export and printing.
- /learn/remember: self-assessed spaced retrieval. Saves text-only explorer questions; omits dependent stimuli rather than saving broken cards.
- Direct contact details share one source in src/lib/sukuunova-contact.ts.
## Model boundaries
Market demand is linear and stock is perishable. Energy storage has no losses. Both are teaching models, not forecasts or engineering plans. Evidence cases distinguish association from proof.
## Question integrity
Coverage identities now ignore decorative wording. Cognitive classifications follow the actual response task. Coverage capacity counts concept/task families, not permutations of names. Foundation review remains supplemental; its existence does not certify university or exam alignment.
## Release requirements
Run full CI and browser smoke; verify touch layout, project reload, direct contacts, audio state and actual listening on target devices.
## Remaining content work
Subject specialists must review university depth and official exam blueprints before claims of full preparation. Large numerical variant capacities elsewhere in the engine are not counts of independently reviewed problems.
## Data boundaries
Public progress/projects are browser-local. They are not automatically published or linked to school records. Cross-device accounts, classroom assignment integration and AI voice coaching need a separate permissions/data design.

## Verified behavior changes
Cosmetic deduplication deliberately returns fewer than a requested 100 questions when a source pool lacks distinct tasks. The old exact-length test was replaced with bounded-size and unique-prompt assertions; padding with duplicates is not allowed.
Adaptive history in the explorer is now scoped by lane, programme and level. Unscoped legacy history remains visible in progress, but does not steer a new level. Foundation recall retains an introductory difficulty even in a later university year.


## Rich-question integrity follow-up

- Difficulty describes the operation being asked, not the learner's class name. A table lookup remains difficulty 1; a range calculation remains difficulty 2 in exam practice.
- Triangle side generators enforce strict triangle inequality. Table maximum questions accept any tied maximum day.
- Comprehension prompts and explanations must be supported by the supplied passage. Changing character names, places or descriptive adjectives does not create a fresh task.
- Mixed maths rotates geometry and data families independently so all ten families can appear.
- The rich generator is school-foundation content, excluded from degree, IELTS and early-years selections. Specialist generators remain responsible for those paths.
- Capacity is a task-family count (geometry 6, tables 4, reading 24 story/task combinations), not a claim of millions of distinct questions. Numeric parameter variants still supply practice.
- Regression tests cover numerical validity, mixed-family coverage, simple exam task calibration, scope exclusions, stable reading identities and table-answer completeness.

These repairs do not certify the remaining question banks or replace specialist curriculum review.
