# Generator relevance and honest variation

Fixes confirmed by code review:
- All-subject intelligent generation now binds each template to an actual subject in the selected programme and year. Previously the subject filter was bypassed for `all`, so unrelated templates could enter the pool.
- Secondary/tertiary intelligent templates are excluded from KG and primary audiences.
- JHS equation and probability generators now recognise the current catalogue topic IDs.
- Stable exposure identities ignore cosmetic people, goods, settings and answer ordering.
- Four contract recognition scenarios and five management recognition scenarios are counted honestly; cosmetic variations no longer increase difficulty or cognitive classification. Contract parties are guaranteed distinct.
- Loop-iteration questions now require inferring iterations from initial value, step and final value instead of stating the answer in the prompt.
- Loop tracing, network transfer, statistics and probability difficulty reflects task structure; explicit rounding instructions accompany network and probability answers.

Limits: this does not make contract questions a complete Ghanaian law syllabus, or certify every course's depth. Other generic fallback banks and capacity formulas still require review. These are routing and assessment-integrity repairs, not regulator endorsement.
